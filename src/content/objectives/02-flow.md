number: 2
title: Evaluate flow-dependent differences
summary: >-
  At identical, pump-controlled infusion rates in 0 g and 1 g, determine whether
  microgravity alters clot lysis time by changing how long the drug actually
  resides at the clot interface.
icon: flow
order: 2
metrics:
  - label: Flow setpoints
    value: 2.5 and 5.0 mL/min
  - label: Flow stability
    value: ±5%
  - label: Primary endpoints
    value: T₂₅, T₅₀ and AUC
  - label: Resolution
    value: ≥5% ΔP in 20 s
---

Holding the pump rate identical in both gravity conditions isolates the variable of
interest. If lysis still differs, the cause is not how fast fluid is being driven
through — it is how gravity changes the local behaviour of that fluid at the clot
face, and therefore how long the drug is in contact with it.

Three measures are computed for each run: time to a 25% and 50% pressure drop
(T₂₅ and T₅₀), the area under the ΔP(t) curve, and a clot lysis rate constant
normalised against the no-drug controls.
