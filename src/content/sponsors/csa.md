The Canadian Space Agency supports the reduced-gravity challenge that puts a
student-built payload on a parabolic flight in the first place. The CSA has
additionally awarded CLOT-LESS a student conference grant supporting presentation
at the International Astronautical Congress, and a student grant covering campaign
travel costs.
