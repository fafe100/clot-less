The Canadian Reduced Gravity Experiment Design Challenge is the competition that
selected CLOT-LESS to fly. CAN-RGX takes student teams from proposal through
design review, safety certification and integration, ending in a parabolic flight
campaign — the structure that makes an experiment like this achievable at the
student level at all.
